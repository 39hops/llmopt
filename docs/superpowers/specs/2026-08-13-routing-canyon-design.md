# Routing canyon flagship design

The flagship animation explains the measured expert-routing intervention as
one continuous change of representation:

`token path -> accumulated demand terrain -> discrete atlas -> selected set
-> matched control -> measured outcome`

The scene opens inside a perspective canyon made from 48 layer profiles. A
real token's eight routed experts ignite at each recorded layer. Real prefill
partial sums then raise the profiles into a demand terrain. The camera moves
overhead and the same 6,144 expert values flatten into a discrete 48 by 128
atlas. Prefill changes to decode without changing normalization, transfer
function, or rank ordering. The 80 pooled-lens carriers lift from their exact
cells as filled light, not annotation rings. One deterministic matched pair
explains the plus-or-minus-eight-rank control construction; the full causal
comparison remains set-level.

## Visual grammar

- Near-black field with data-derived inferno color and warm-white carriers.
- Data occupies the frame. Perspective and camera motion provide depth.
- No opening sentence, explanatory paragraph, white outline cloud, table,
  or provenance over live data.
- Typography is limited to phase labels, the matched-pair construction, and
  the final measured outcome.
- The final outcome is a clean three-point comparison on black. Provenance
  follows on an isolated, readable card.

## Honesty contract

- Every terrain vertex and atlas cell represents one recorded expert slot.
- Prefill and decode use one shared maximum and transfer function.
- Column order stays the pooled per-layer demand rank used by the sampler.
- Perspective depth, trail decay, camera movement, and flattening are
  illustrative. Expert identities and all endpoint values are analytic.
- No interpolation invents experts or post-deletion routing trajectories.
- The close-up pairing is descriptive of control construction only.
- The `+28`, `+55`, and `+27` results apply to complete deletion sets.
